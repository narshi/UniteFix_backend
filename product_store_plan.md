## 1. Where you are today (what the code already does)

The most important finding: **most of the store already exists, in two half-connected halves.**

| | Legacy shop | Marketplace ("Partner Hub phase 7") |
|---|---|---|
| Routes | `/api/products`, `/api/cart/*`, `/api/orders/place` | `/api/store/*`, `/api/hub/store/*` |
| Tables | `products`, `cart_items`, `product_orders`, `return_requests` | `market_checkouts`, `seller_orders`, `seller_order_items`, `seller_order_events`, `product_reviews` |
| Used by the mobile app | **Yes** (`mobile/src/api/shop.api.ts`) | **No** (nothing in the app calls `/api/store/*`) |
| Seller | UniteFix only | Partners, each with their own order; UniteFix itself is refused (`NOT_MARKETPLACE`) |
| Strengths | Simple | GST per line, invoice and credit note (`taxDocuments`), commission, TCS report, penalties for late dispatch, return approval, verified-purchase reviews, settlement after the return window, Delhivery booking and label |
| Weaknesses | Cart has no variant; checkout reads a column `stock_quantity` that `schema.ts` doesn't define (probably dead); return window hard-coded to 24 h (`return.service.ts`) | No server-side cart, no search, no tracking timeline, no recommendations, no rating aggregates, stock is taken only **after** payment |

Other facts that shape the plan:

- **The shop tab is switched off.** `AI_CONTEXT.md` §3.K says product ordering is halted and every shop screen shows `ComingSoonOverlay`. Un-halting is a decision to record there.
- **Collections use Razorpay.** Cashfree appears only for payouts (`cashfree.service.ts`, `payout-sync.service.ts`).
- **Courier today is Delhivery**, in `mock` mode unless `DELHIVERY_MODE=live`. There is no India Post code at all.
- **Background work is in-process timers** (`setInterval` in `task_queues.ts`). Fine for one instance; unsafe if you ever run two.
- **Return window** exists per product (`returnWindowDays`, default 7) and is copied onto each seller order. There is no admin-configured global or per-category default.
- Already built and reusable: product variants table, product images, brands and categories, `mrp`, `hsnCode`, `gstPercent`, `netQuantity`, `countryOfOrigin`, `manufacturer` (Legal Metrology fields), `warrantyMonths`, stock decrement guarded by `stock >= qty`, idempotent payment capture with a row lock, sold-out-after-payment auto-refund.

**Recommendation:** do not build a third store. Converge on the marketplace model and retire the legacy cart path.

## 2. Recommended decisions (own + partner model)

_Updated 2026-11-25. These are my recommendations; anything marked **VERIFY** needs your CA or lawyer before launch._

### 2.1 One pipeline, two kinds of seller

Every sale goes through the marketplace pipeline (checkout → one order per seller → invoice → settlement). UniteFix becomes **seller #0**: a seller profile backed by UniteFix's own GSTIN, address and grievance officer. The only differences are flags, not code paths:

| | UniteFix (first-party) | Partner (third-party) |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice issued by | UniteFix, own series `UF/26-27/S00001` | The partner, in their name and GSTIN, generated by the platform (exists: `bp-<id>-inv`) |
| GST TCS (sec. 52) | None (own supply) | Collected by UniteFix as e-commerce operator (exists: `tcsPaise`, `tcsReport`) |
| Income-tax TDS (sec. 194-O) | None | Deducted by UniteFix (exists: `tdsPaise`) **VERIFY current rate** |
| Commission / fees | None | Monthly fee invoice from UniteFix to the partner (exists: `issueFeeInvoice`) |
| Settlement | Stays with UniteFix | After the return window, net of fees, TCS, TDS (exists: `settleDue`) |
| Stock | UniteFix warehouse | Partner's own |

A mixed cart (a UniteFix laptop plus a partner's spices) is still **one payment** that splits into **two orders, two invoices, two parcels**. The app must say so before payment ("2 sellers, 2 deliveries, 2 invoices").

**VERIFY:** an e-commerce company with foreign investment may not sell its own inventory. If UniteFix is wholly Indian-owned this does not apply.

### 2.2 Who sells what

- **UniteFix's own range:** computers and accessories, plus dry fruits and spices **only when bought already packed and labelled** from licensed suppliers. Repacking loose dry fruits or spices yourself needs your own FSSAI licence for the premises and Legal Metrology packer registration, so don't repack at launch.
- **Partners:** home-made food and regional spices, sold by the people who make them. The maker's FSSAI registration and label obligations stay with the maker. UniteFix verifies them before listing and shows the FSSAI number on the listing and invoice.
- **Recommended:** home food from partners, not from your own kitchen. It keeps food-safety liability with a registered maker and needs no kitchen licence from UniteFix.

### 2.3 Sellers by GST status (needed for home cooks)

Today `readiness()` refuses any seller without a GSTIN. Most home cooks won't have one, so add a seller **GST type**:

| GST type | Allowed sales | Document to the customer | TCS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular (GSTIN) | Anywhere in India | Tax invoice | Yes |
| Composition | **Within the seller's own state only** **VERIFY** | Bill of supply, with "composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax" | **VERIFY** |
| Unregistered (PAN + GST enrolment number) | **Within the seller's own state only**, under the 2023 rules allowing unregistered sellers on e-commerce **VERIFY the notification and conditions** | Sale receipt without GST (not a tax invoice) | No |

The quote step enforces this: an in-state-only seller's items can't be bought for a delivery pincode in another state. Store the enrolment number and declared address on the seller, and show the right document type automatically.

### 2.4 Payments and partner money

- **Stay on Razorpay for collections.** Checkout, payment links, refunds, the webhook and the capture logic already use it. Switching gateways adds risk for no gain right now.
- **Partner shares should flow through the gateway, not through UniteFix's bank account.** Today a partner's sale goes to UniteFix's account, is credited to the partner's ledger, and is paid out later by Cashfree. RBI's payment aggregator rules may treat a marketplace that holds sellers' money in its own account as acting as an aggregator. The usual fix is the gateway's split-settlement feature (Razorpay Route, or Cashfree Easy Split if you consolidate on Cashfree), released after the return window. **VERIFY with your CA and with Razorpay before partner sales go live.** UniteFix's own sales are unaffected.
- Coupons: a **seller-funded** discount appears on that seller's invoice and lowers the taxable value. A **UniteFix-funded** discount on a partner's item does not change the partner's invoice; record it as UniteFix's marketing cost. **VERIFY** the GST treatment with your CA before running platform-funded offers on partner items. At launch, allow only seller-funded coupons and UniteFix coupons on UniteFix's own items.

### 2.5 Return defaults (admin-editable)

| Range | Change of mind | Damaged / wrong / defective on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| Computers and accessories (UniteFix) | 2 days, seal and packaging intact | 2 days; after that, the warranty |
| Computers and accessories (partner) | Partner picks 0–2 days (admin sets the cap) | 2 days |
| Dry fruits, spices | Non-returnable | 48 h, photo required |
| Home-made food | Non-returnable | 24 h, photo required |

The policy is shown on the listing before purchase and frozen onto the order. A partner can be stricter than the cap, never looser.

### 2.6 Other calls

- **India Post:** manual article numbers at launch; start the official onboarding with your India Post Circle office now, since it takes weeks.
- **Launch order:** UniteFix computers first; then UniteFix packed dry fruits and spices; then partner spices; then partner home food last, only after FSSAI checks, batch and expiry records, and shelf-life checks are live.
- **Shipping charge:** charged by the seller who ships and included on that seller's invoice. UniteFix's courier booking fee to a partner stays a separate fee invoice (exists). **VERIFY** the rate applied to the shipping line with your CA.

## 3. Target architecture

**Principle: a modular monolith, hardened, not microservices.** You have one team, one Postgres, one Render service. Splitting into services now would add cost and failure modes without any gain. Design clean module boundaries so a module can be lifted out later.

```
Mobile app / web  →  API (Express)  →  Postgres (primary)
                          │                   ├─ read replica (when needed)
                          ├─ Outbox table ──▶ Job worker (pg-boss on Postgres)
                          │                   ├─ notifications, invoices, emails
                          │                   ├─ carrier tracking poller
                          │                   └─ recommendation / stats refresh
                          ├─ Razorpay webhook (idempotent)
                          └─ Cloudinary / CDN for images
```

### Modules (folders under `server/services/store/`)

| Module | Owns |
|---|---|
| `catalog` | products, variants, categories, regions, attributes, images, search |
| `inventory` | stock ledger, reservations |
| `cart` | server-side cart, buy-now session |
| `checkout` | quote, address, shipping charge, coupon, payment start |
| `orders` | order state machine, events, cancellation |
| `fulfilment` | carrier adapters, labels, tracking |
| `returns` | policy resolution, return requests, refunds |
| `billing` | invoices, credit notes (reuses `taxDocuments`) |
| `reviews` | ratings, aggregates, moderation |
| `discovery` | recommendations, recently viewed, wishlist |

Rule: modules call each other only through their exported service functions, never through each other's tables.

### Scale posture (be honest about the numbers)

Design for roughly **10× your first year**, not for a million users. A reasonable first target: 50 k SKUs, 5 k orders/day, 50 req/s peak, p95 under 300 ms on reads. Postgres plus HTTP caching handles that. Add Redis, a search engine or a replica only when a measured limit says so (triggers in §10).

## 4. Data model changes (additive only)

Existing columns stay. All changes are `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS` / new tables, following the `hub-migrations.ts` pattern. No data is rewritten.

**Catalog**
- `products`: `kind` (`electronics` | `food` | `spice` | `dryfruit`), `region_id`, `shelf_life_days`, `fssai_number`, `is_perishable`, `return_policy_id` (nullable override), `search_vector` (tsvector, generated).
- `product_regions` (id, name, state, gi_tag, story, image): "Malabar pepper", "Kashmiri saffron", "Guntur chilli"…
- `product_variants`: already has sku, price, mrp, stock, attributes. Add `weight_grams` (needed for postage), `pack_label` ("250 g"), `barcode`. Computers use RAM/SSD attributes; dry fruits and spices use pack sizes.
- `product_batches` (variant_id, batch_no, mfg_date, expiry_date, qty): **required for food**, optional for the rest. The order line stores the batch shipped, which gives you recall and the shelf-life check.

**Inventory**
- `stock_ledger` (variant_id, delta, reason, ref_type, ref_id, created_at): append-only; `stock` becomes a derived/cached column.
- `stock_reservations` (checkout_id, variant_id, qty, expires_at): placed when payment starts, released on expiry or failure, converted on capture. This fixes today's "paid, then sold out" refund path.

**Cart / buy now**
- `carts` (user_id, updated_at) and `cart_lines` (cart_id, variant_id, qty, price_seen_paise, added_at). The server re-prices at checkout; the stored price is only used to tell the customer "price changed".
- Buy now creates an ephemeral checkout with one line and does **not** touch the cart.

**Orders** (extend, don't replace)
- `seller_orders`: add `shipping_charge_paise`, `coupon_paise`, `carrier`, `awb`, `tracking_status`, `tracking_updated_at`, `delivered_confirmed_by` (carrier | customer | system), `return_policy_snapshot` (jsonb: the policy that applied at purchase).
- `shipment_events` (seller_order_id, carrier_status_raw, normalized_status, location, event_at, source): the tracking timeline.
- `order_idempotency` (user_id, key, response, created_at): replay-safe checkout.

**Returns**
- `return_policies` (id, name, mode `none` | `window`, window_days, damage_claim_hours, applies_to `global` | `category` | `kind`, ref_id, is_active).
- Extend the marketplace return flow with a `claim_type` (`change_of_mind` | `damaged` | `wrong_item` | `defective`) and `photos`.

**Reviews / discovery**
- `product_rating_stats` (product_id, count, avg, histogram jsonb), refreshed by a job, so the product page reads one row.
- `product_events` (user_id, product_id, type `view|cart|buy`, at), append-only, partitioned monthly.
- `product_cooccurrence` (a, b, score), rebuilt nightly.
- `wishlists` (user_id, product_id).

## 5. Return policy engine (admin-configurable)

**Resolution order** (first match wins), evaluated at purchase and frozen into `return_policy_snapshot`:

1. Product override (`products.return_policy_id`)
2. Category / `kind` default (e.g. food = non-returnable)
3. Global default (initial value: 2 days)

**Two separate claims, because the law treats them differently:**

| Claim | Rule | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Change of mind | Window counted from delivery; none for non-returnable items | Global 2 days; food and opened items: none |
| Damaged / wrong / defective on arrival | Always allowed within a short window, photo required | 48 h from delivery |

"Non-returnable" must never block a damaged-on-arrival claim; selling food as "no returns at all" is the quickest way to a consumer-forum complaint **VERIFY with your lawyer**.

**Admin page: `Store → Return rules`:** global default, per-kind and per-category rows, per-product override on the product editor, a preview ("a customer buying this today gets: …"), and an audit entry for every change. Existing orders keep the policy they were sold under.

**Window clock:** starts at confirmed delivery (carrier event or customer confirmation), not at dispatch. The existing `settleAfter = deliveredAt + window` logic keeps working, so seller settlement and the return window stay linked.

**Refund mechanics:** reuse `initiateRefund` and the refund-status sync; add credit note generation on every approved return (the marketplace flow already issues one).

## 6. Tracking and India Post

**What I found (VERIFY directly with India Post):**
- There is **no public self-serve tracking API**. India Post's own onboarding document describes a bulk-customer route: your Circle sales contact raises an integration request, the CEPT team sets up testing, and the services listed include article data sharing at booking, event data sharing from India Post back to you, and a tariff API. ([India Post / CEPT document](https://utilities.cept.gov.in/dop/pdfbind.ashx?id=5778))
- Commercial aggregators sell India Post / Speed Post tracking APIs with batch lookups and webhooks, for example [TrackingMore](https://www.trackingmore.com/india-post-tracking-api) and [Ship24](https://ship24.com/couriers/speed-post-tracking). I have not confirmed pricing or accuracy.
- I would **not** scrape the public tracking page: it is fragile, has CAPTCHAs, and may breach terms.

**Design: a carrier adapter, so the carrier is a plug-in:**

```ts
interface CarrierAdapter {
  id: 'india_post' | 'delhivery' | 'manual';
  book?(order, parcel): Promise<{ awb: string; label?: Buffer }>;   // optional
  track(awb: string): Promise<NormalizedEvent[]>;
  cancel?(awb: string): Promise<void>;
}
```

`NormalizedEvent.status` is one of: `booked → picked_up → in_transit → out_for_delivery → delivered | delivery_failed | returned_to_sender`. Raw text is kept alongside.

**India Post rollout in three steps** (each is shippable on its own):

| Step | What | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| A. Manual | Seller (or staff) enters the 13-character Speed Post article number when dispatching. The customer sees "Dispatched via India Post" with a link-out to India Post's tracking page and the number to copy. The `manual` adapter. | Small |
| B. Aggregator | The `india_post` adapter calls a tracking aggregator. A job polls open shipments (every 2–4 h for in-transit, hourly when out for delivery, then stops) and/or receives webhooks. Status and the timeline update automatically. | Medium |
| C. Official | Replace the aggregator with India Post's article-data and event-data feeds once onboarded. Same adapter interface, nothing else changes. | Medium + waiting time |

**Tracking behaviour:**
- Timeline in the order screen from `shipment_events`; push notification on dispatched, out for delivery, delivered, failed.
- **Stuck-shipment alert:** no new event for N days, or past an expected-delivery date, raises an admin task.
- **Delivered** starts the return window. If the carrier never reports delivery, a job marks it delivered after a configurable number of days and asks the customer to confirm.
- Reuse `store-shipping.service.ts` for Delhivery as the second adapter.

**Food and India Post (VERIFY locally):** I found no official food list. A secondary source says dry fruits, nuts and powdered spices generally go through, while liquids, cooked or perishable food, and strong-smelling items are refused ([Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/food/features/2025/12/09/avoid-food-parcel-rejection.html); [India Post prohibited items](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/mailproducts/prohibited-items)). Acceptance can depend on the local office. So: ship **only shelf-stable dry items** by post at launch, and keep pickles, sweets with ghee or anything wet out until you have a courier that accepts it.

## 7. Checkout, payments and invoice

**Flow (every step idempotent):**

1. `POST /api/store/cart/*` (add, update, remove, merge).
2. `POST /api/store/quote`: re-prices from the database; returns lines, GST per line, shipping (by weight and pincode), coupon, total. Also returns warnings (price changed, low stock, shelf-life too short, **pincode not serviceable by post**).
3. `POST /api/store/checkout` with an `Idempotency-Key` header: creates the checkout, **reserves stock** for 15 minutes, creates the Razorpay order.
4. Payment captured → webhook **and** the app's verify call both land on `applyCapture` (already row-locked and idempotent). It converts reservations into the stock ledger, creates seller orders, enqueues invoice and notifications through the outbox.
5. Reservation expiry job releases abandoned carts' stock.

**Why reservations:** today stock is taken after payment. With two buyers on the last item, one pays and is refunded. Reservations remove that for normal traffic.

**Reconciliation job (already partly there):** every few minutes, any checkout in `pending` for more than N minutes is checked against the gateway, so a lost webhook never leaves a paid customer without an order.

**Shipping charge:** a rate table in admin by weight slab and zone (local / state / national), with a free-shipping threshold. India Post tariffs are weight and distance based; start with your own slab table and replace it with the tariff API in step C.

**Invoice:**
- Reuse `issueSellerInvoice` and `taxDocuments`: one GST invoice per seller order, credit note on return. Today the invoice is issued at dispatch; keep that.
- Add: FSSAI number and batch/expiry on food invoices, serial number on computer invoices, "download PDF" in the app, invoice email.
- E-invoicing (IRN) applies above a turnover threshold; `einvoice.service.ts` is a 34-line stub. Ask your CA whether you cross it **VERIFY**.
- The e-commerce operator TCS report already exists (`tcsReport`); keep it for partner sales.

## 7A. Invoice compliance (every document the store issues)

**Already right in the code (keep):** consecutive numbering per series per financial year, at most 16 characters, allocated atomically; CGST+SGST vs IGST by place of supply; amounts frozen at issue, corrections only by credit note; partner invoices in the partner's own name and series with the operator's GSTIN noted; credit note on an accepted return; installation as a separate service line with its own SAC.

**The document set:**

| Event | Document | Issued by | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer pays (before dispatch) | Receipt voucher (no tax on advances for goods) | The seller | **Add** (the document kind exists) **VERIFY** your CA wants it for prepaid goods |
| Order cancelled before dispatch | Refund voucher against that receipt voucher | The seller | **Add** (kind exists) |
| Dispatch | Tax invoice (regular seller) / bill of supply (composition) / sale receipt (unregistered) | The seller | Tax invoice exists; **add** the other two |
| UniteFix's own sale | Tax invoice in UniteFix's series | UniteFix | **Add** (`uf-store` series) |
| Return accepted | Credit note referencing the original invoice | The seller | Exists for partners; **add** for UniteFix |
| Price corrected upward | Debit note | The seller | **Add** (rare) |
| Monthly fees to partners | Tax invoice for commission, gateway fee, courier, penalties | UniteFix | Exists |
| Travel trip advance (a service) | Receipt voucher **with GST**, then a tax invoice at the trip | UniteFix | **Gap today:** trip advances issue no document. Fix with this work |

**Fields every invoice must carry** (check against CGST Rule 46 with your CA):
supplier name, address and GSTIN; serial number and date; recipient name and delivery address (name and address mandatory on B2C invoices of ₹50,000 or more); place of supply with state name and code; HSN code per line (4 or 6 digits depending on turnover); description, quantity and unit; taxable value after seller-funded discount; GST rate and CGST/SGST/IGST amounts; whether reverse charge applies; signature (a digital or "computer-generated" statement as your CA advises).

**Store-specific additions:**
- **"Sold by / Shipped by"** on every invoice and in the app, with the seller's grievance officer (consumer e-commerce rules; partly exists).
- **Food:** FSSAI number of the maker, batch number, best-before date.
- **Computers:** serial number captured at dispatch, warranty terms and who honours them.
- **B2B buyers:** an optional "Buying for business? Add GSTIN" at checkout. The invoice then carries the buyer's GSTIN, and place of supply follows the buyer's state. If the seller's turnover is above the e-invoicing threshold, the invoice needs an IRN before issue; `einvoice.service.ts` is a stub, so pick a GSP provider before taking B2B orders from large sellers.
- **E-way bill:** a consignment above ₹50,000 by road normally needs one (laptops can cross this). Whether India Post parcels are exempt is unclear, and Delhivery shipments are not exempt. **VERIFY** before shipping high-value laptops; build the e-way bill number field on the shipment now.
- **Returns paperwork:** the credit note must be issued within the GST time limit for the financial year **VERIFY the deadline with your CA**, so auto-issue it the moment a return is accepted, as today.

**Reporting:**
- Sellers report their sales made through the operator in their own returns; give every partner a monthly downloadable sales and invoice register.
- UniteFix files the operator's TCS return from the existing TCS report.
- The existing GST desk (`gst-desk.service.ts`) already handles receipt and refund vouchers; extend it to the new store series.

**Build rule:** the invoice is generated from the frozen order lines and the seller's GST profile at the moment of issue, never from the current product row. A product price edit after the sale can then never change an invoice.

## 8. Ratings, order history, discovery

**Ratings and reviews** (the table exists: verified-purchase, one per order line, seller reply):
- Add `product_rating_stats` so listings show stars without scanning reviews.
- Prompt for a review 3 days after delivery (notification), with photo upload and a helpful-vote count.
- Moderation queue in admin (hide, restore, report); rate-limit and one review per order line (already enforced).
- Seller rating (exists: `metrics`) feeds search ranking and the "sold by" badge.

**Order history:** `GET /api/store/orders` exists. Add: filters (status, date), search by product, reorder button (adds the lines to the cart at current prices), invoice and tracking shortcuts, cursor pagination.

**Wishlist and recently viewed:** small tables; the recent list can live on-device first.

**Recommendations: grow in stages, no ML platform needed**

| Stage | Method | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Popular in category, new arrivals, "more from this region", "more from this seller" (pure SQL, cached 10 min) | Launch |
| 2 | "Frequently bought together" from `product_cooccurrence` (nightly job over paid orders); "Because you bought…" from the user's own history; recently viewed | After ~1–2 k orders |
| 3 | Re-rank with recency, margin and stock; seasonal boosts (festival sweets, monsoon); embeddings of titles/descriptions in `pgvector` for "similar items" | When Stage 2 plateaus |

Cold start (new users, new items) falls back to Stage 1. Every recommendation click is logged to `product_events` so you can measure uplift before investing further. Never recommend out-of-stock or paused items.

**Search:** Postgres full-text (`search_vector`) plus `pg_trgm` for typos and Kannada/Hindi transliterations at launch. Filters: category, kind, region, price, brand, rating, in stock. Move to Typesense or Meilisearch when queries exceed a few hundred ms or you need better relevance. Keep the search behind one `catalog.search()` function so the swap is local.

## 10. Scalability, reliability and security (the architect's checklist)

| Concern | Now | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Reads | Direct DB | `Cache-Control` + CDN for catalog and images; 60 s in-process cache for hot lists; add Redis only if cache must be shared across instances |
| Writes | Direct | Outbox pattern: the order transaction writes an `outbox` row; a worker sends notifications and creates invoices, so the customer's request never waits on them and never loses them |
| Jobs | `setInterval` in the web process | Move to **pg-boss** (queue on your existing Postgres): retries, backoff, dedupe, runs safely with 2+ instances. No new infrastructure |
| Stock | Decrement at capture | Ledger + reservations (§4); `UPDATE … WHERE stock >= qty` stays as the final guard |
| Duplicate requests | 60 s duplicate check in legacy path | `Idempotency-Key` on checkout, return, cancel, refund |
| Payments | Webhook + verify, row lock | Add the reconciliation sweep (§7) and alert on any checkout `pending > 30 min` |
| Search | None | Postgres FTS → Typesense when needed |
| Images | Cloudinary | Keep; serve resized variants; lazy-load in lists |
| Pagination | Offset | Cursor pagination on orders, products, reviews |
| Events table | New | Monthly partitions; drop or roll up after 13 months |
| Observability | `logger` | Request id on every log line; metrics for checkout success rate, payment→order latency, stuck shipments, queue depth; alert on error-rate and queue-age |

**Triggers to scale (measure, then act):**
- Read replica when primary CPU stays above ~60% from catalog reads.
- Dedicated search when search p95 exceeds 300 ms or relevance complaints appear.
- Redis when you run more than one API instance and need a shared cache or rate-limit store.
- Split the worker into its own Render service when queue age exceeds a minute during normal load.

## 13. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| India Post status is slow or inconsistent | Customer-confirmed delivery fallback; stuck-shipment alerts; insurance/registered service for high-value computers |
| Food sold without valid FSSAI or short shelf life | Hard blocks in the listing and quote steps, not just warnings |
| Parcel refused or damaged in post | Packaging guide per category; damage claims always allowed |
| Overselling / double payment | Reservations, ledger, idempotency keys, reconciliation |
| Fake or abusive reviews | Verified-purchase only, rate limits, moderation queue |
| Seller non-fulfilment | Existing late-dispatch penalties, seller scorecard, auto-cancel after SLA |
| Scope creep from three product families | Launch order in §2.6; one family per release |
| Two order systems drift apart | Phase 0 retires the legacy path |
| Partner money held in UniteFix's account | Gateway split settlement for partner shares (§2.4), confirmed with CA and gateway before partner launch |
| Wrong document type for a seller's GST status | Document type chosen from the seller's GST profile at issue; in-state-only sellers blocked at quote |
| Trip advances without GST documents | Receipt voucher with GST for travel advances, built alongside the store vouchers |
